# F3, F4: Proposition 5.3 per cell, checked in Lean 4

Status (2026-10-17): every one of the 91,273 stored cells is verified to be a single-term exclusion (Lemma 5.1),
a cell whose core polynomials are certified initial forms and the generators of a cofactor certificate (Lemma 5.2),
or one of the 43 listed open cells. Clean build of all F3/F4 checks: 48.3 s wall, 9 min 12 s CPU on a box with 384
vCPUs (`build.log` lines 717–720), the longest chunk module 40 s (`chunk_times.tsv`).

## What is checked

`L6Check/F34/C<N>K<k>.lean`, 123 theorems `L6.F34.Checks.F34_c<N>_k<k>` (generated by `scripts/f34/gen_checks.py`):

```
checkChunk N n6 cells_N single_N links_N pass_N open43 [(certid, L6.Certs.C<certid>), …] lo hi
  { single := s, core1 := c1, core2 := c2, opn := o } = true      -- by native_decide
```

`checkChunk` (`L6F34/Check.lean`) parses the embedded files (`prepare`) and checks every cell `lo ≤ idx < hi` of
`data/cells/c<N>.jsonl` (`cellKind ≠ 0`): the cell is in exactly one of three lists, and

* **single term** (F3, `data/f34/single/c<N>.txt`, identity + term `i`): the identity is one of §5.1 (torques `T_J`
  with `0 ∈ J`, `|J| ≤ 5`; subset sums with `2 ≤ |J| ≤ 4`; `Ip`; partial virials; differences; `UI`), and for every
  other term `k` the system `Π + {e_k − e_i ≤ 0}` has a certificate accepted by `Farkas.check` (Lemma 4.6), where `Π`
  is the cell (`tsec.cell_lp`: the rows of `tengine.base_lp` over all points and the argmin rows of the base
  identities, built here independently of F2's code, data times 2) and the exponent forms are those of
  `tsec.secondary`;
* **core** (F4, `data/f4/links/c<N>.txt`: certificate, fixed mass `F`, names, renaming): the certificate (read by
  `L6.Poly.parseCert`, the reading of the cofactor checks) has the link's variables and `F`, and its generator `i`
  equals the initial form of name `i` computed from the cell: rows `D_kl² z_kl w_kl − 1`; base identities on the
  cell's own argmin set; secondary identities on an argmin set `S` guessed at a floating-point point of `Π` and
  certified constant (accepted certificates for `Π + {±(e_j − e_{i₀}) ≥ s}`, `j ∈ S`, and `Π + {e_k − e_{i₀} ≤ 0}`,
  `k ∉ S`); the coefficients follow `tinit.system` and `tsec.term_expr`; the form is multiplied by the lcm of its
  denominators (2 for a partial virial with a `½` term), then `m_F := 1` if `F ≥ 0`, normalized, and compared exactly
  (sparse polynomials of `L6Poly.Basic`);
* **open**: the cell is listed in `data/cells/open43.tsv`;
* in addition (a guard, not needed for soundness) the encoded cell has a floating-point point with `s > 1e-9`.

The counts of the kinds are part of each statement. Further theorems:

* `F34_cover` (`Cover.lean`): the numbers of cells of the 191 classes (`numCells`), by `native_decide`;
* `F34_chunks_cover` (`Main.lean`): the chunks of each class are consecutive from 0 to its number of cells
  (`decide +kernel`);
* `F34_totals` (`Main.lean`): the sums of the counts of the 123 chunks (`decide +kernel`);
* `F34_sha_cells`, `F34_sha_n6` (`Sha.lean`): the SHA-256 of the 191 embedded cell lists equal
  `data/cells/SHA256SUMS` (the engine's `cells.jsonl`), and that of the embedded `n6.json` is
  `0f2ae5d0e1e8619eeb680c12b04fb168faaa856e43d61b16074df50a874e2ff4`.

## Meaning, proved (`L6Sound/F34/Meaning.lean`, no `sorry`; axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`,
`meaning_build.log`)

* `checkChunk_spec`: a passing chunk parses (`prepare … = some cd`), `lo ≤ hi ≤ cd.ncells`, every cell
  `lo ≤ idx < hi` has `cellKind cd idx ≠ 0`, and the counts are the stated ones;
* `cellKind_spec`: such a cell is in exactly one list (`kindsOf cd idx = 1`) and is open, or has a single term with
  `checkSingle x rows nm lab = true` for its parsed cell, or has a link whose certificate is among the given ones with
  `checkLink … = true`;
* `checkSingle_spec`: the identity's term list contains the term `i`, and for every other term `k`,
  `Farkas.check nh (rows.push (rowLe ts k i)) (LP.decide …).cert = true`;
* `checkLink_spec`, `genFor_spec`, `genBase_spec`, `genSec_spec`, `constantArgmin_spec`: the generators are the
  normalized initial forms of the names; for a secondary identity the argmin set is `S = i₀ :: rest` with accepted
  certificates as above;
* `parseAll_lookup`, `prepare_spec`: each certificate used is `parseCert` of one of the given texts
  `L6.Certs.C<certid>` (the constants that the cofactor checks use), and the open cells are those of `open43.tsv`.

Combined with Lemma 4.6 (`Farkas.check` sound; `L6Sound/Common/Farkas.lean`; the compositions are in
`L6Sound/Composed.lean`: `L6.F34.single_strict`, `constantArgmin_strict`, `core_cell_excluded`, `chunk_meaning`, and
for all chunks `L6.F34.Checks.F34_all_meaning` in `L6Check/F34/AllCerts.lean`): every accepted certificate excludes
every point of the system with `s > 0`; hence for a single-term cell, `e_i < e_k` on all of `Π` for `k ≠ i` (the
added row is not strict), i.e. the hypothesis of Lemma 5.1; for a secondary identity of a core, `e_j = e_{i₀}` on `Π`
for `j ∈ S` and `e_k > e_{i₀}` for `k ∉ S`, i.e. its argmin set is constant on the cell. Combined with the soundness of
the cofactor checks (`checkCert L6.Certs.C<certid> = true`, `L6.Poly.checkCert_sound`): a common zero of the cell's core polynomials
(with `m_F = 1`) makes `h(m) = 0`, with `h(3,5,7,11,13,17) ≠ 0`, the hypothesis of Lemma 5.2. The framework (places,
exponents, initial forms, Lemmas 5.1, 5.2) is stated in the paper, not formalized.

## Axioms (`axioms.log`)

Of the 128 theorems of `L6Check/F34`, the 123 chunk theorems and `F34_cover` depend on `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` and their own auxiliary axiom `<theorem>._native.native_decide.ax_1_1`;
`F34_sha_cells`, `F34_sha_n6` on `propext`, `Quot.sound` and their auxiliary axiom; `F34_chunks_cover` on `propext`
only; `F34_totals` on no axiom. No `sorryAx`, no other axiom.
