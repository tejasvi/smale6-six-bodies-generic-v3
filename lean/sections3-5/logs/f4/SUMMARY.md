## Reproduce

```
python3 scripts/f4/collect.py IN_DIR RUN_DIR
python3 scripts/f4/lift.py RUN_DIR --jobs 200 --timeout 1800 --memcap 64 --minfree 80 --tag sweep1
python3 scripts/f4/lift.py RUN_DIR --jobs 100 --timeout 1800 --method elim --tag sweep2_elim
python3 scripts/f4/lift.py RUN_DIR --jobs 100 --timeout 1800 --method liftstd_slimgb --tag sweep2_liftstd_slimgb
python3 scripts/f4/altcore.py SYSTEMS.jsonl RUN_DIR --tries 8 --seed 1
python3 scripts/f4/altcore.py SYSTEMS.jsonl RUN_DIR --tries 24 --t0 8 --bias 3 --seed 1 --out cand_cores2.jsonl
python3 scripts/f4/lift.py RUN_DIR --cores RUN_DIR/data/f4/cand_cores.jsonl --timeout 600 --method liftstd_slimgb
python3 scripts/f4/lift.py RUN_DIR --only @BIG1.txt --force --timeout 600 --method liftstd_slimgb  # files > 1 MB
python3 scripts/f4/lift.py RUN_DIR --only @BIG2.txt --force --timeout 600 --method elim            # files > 1 MB
python3 scripts/f4/lift.py RUN_DIR --only @BIG3.txt --force --timeout 600 --varorder rev           # files > 2 MB
python3 scripts/f4/lift.py RUN_DIR --only @BIG3.txt --force --timeout 600 --varorder rev --method liftstd_slimgb
python3 scripts/f4/choose.py RUN_DIR HARDCELLS.txt
python3 scripts/f4/collect.py IN_DIR RUN_DIR --replace RUN_DIR/data/f4/replacements.jsonl
python3 scripts/f4/prune.py RUN_DIR
python3 scripts/f4/check.py IN_DIR RUN_DIR --replace RUN_DIR/data/f4/replacements.jsonl --systems SYSTEMS.jsonl
python3 scripts/f4/dropone.py RUN_DIR IDS.txt
python3 scripts/f4/summary.py RUN_DIR
```

The runs and their parameters are recorded in the `tag`, `method`, `order`, `varorder` and `timeout` fields of
`logs/f4/lift.jsonl`. Several runs overlapped on the same cores (the smaller verified file is kept). The run
`sweep1` was stopped before its time limit, while 42 of its lifts (`lift/dp/canon`) were still running, after
about 21 to 24 minutes; those 42 lifts left no record, and each of their cores was attempted by the
other runs. HARDCELLS.txt lists the cells whose stored core had no certificate after the runs sweep1, sweep2_*,
hard9_* and pilot4_* (lines `class cell certid F` of the links); SYSTEMS.jsonl holds their initial systems,
taken from `exact6/c<N>/sec/p*.sec.jsonl` (the records with key `system`).
